# Purpose

What nefzen.dev is for, and who it's for. New content and projects should fit this.

## Goals

1. **Show the work.** A personal site about me and the things I make, in one consistent, polished look.
2. **Link to every project.** Each project gets a crawlable link from here, so search engines find and index it.

## Audience

- People considering working with me, coming from my CV.
- People who found something I made and want to see more.
- Supporters, later on.

## Content

The site says who I am, what I do, and why the site exists. It complements a CV rather than repeating it.

- 14+ years of software development in the industry.
- Leading in frontend and web technologies.
- What I make now: web experiences and code factories.
- A short, personal about section.
- The projects in "Things I made".
